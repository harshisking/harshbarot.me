A Tale of Power

Last Updated: 28/03/2026 Status: Complete 2 min read
I am to tell a story,
Of utter power and glory.
Be its origin light or dark,
It leaves a bleeding mark.
Men came,
Men made,
Men eroded,
Men—dead.
What story am I to tell?
A tale of power and prowess—
Dark, perhaps—yet fiction still,
An insane writer’s imagination.
Proclaimed are the fathers of this art,
Whose words—
Pierce through the flesh of the mind,
Like a dart.
~
